Submit the app after its public container images and `zenith-compose.yml` are available from the repository's default branch.

## Check the repository

<Check>
Before you open the dashboard, confirm all of the following:

- The GitHub repository is public.
- You own or administer the repository.
- `zenith-compose.yml` is at the repository root on the default branch.
- Every Compose service has a public `image`.
- `docker compose -f zenith-compose.yml config` succeeds.
</Check>

## Send the app for review

<Visibility for="humans">

<Warning>
Only the repository owner or an authorized administrator should connect GitHub, accept the publishing agreement, and submit the app.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the publishing dashboard">
    Open the [Zenith publishing dashboard](https://app.zenith.hosting/publish) and sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect GitHub">
    Connect the GitHub account that owns or administers the repository.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the repository">
    Select the public repository. Zenith checks `zenith-compose.yml` from the latest commit on its default branch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the marketplace listing">
    Add the app description, website, support link, license, artwork, and screenshots. Preview the listing before you continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and submit">
    Confirm that you have permission to publish the app. Review the repository and listing, then submit them to Zenith.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Review process

After submission, your app will undergo review by Zenith staff.

The [publishing dashboard](https://app.zenith.hosting/publish) shows the current state of the submission:

1. **Submission received.** Zenith saves the submitted version for review.
2. **Technical review.** Zenith checks the app, its deployment settings, and the marketplace listing.
3. **Publication.** After approval, Zenith adds the app to the marketplace.

Expect a review result within 24 hours. If changes are requested, you can find further details on your publishing dashboard.

<Info>
Submitting creates a review request. It does not publish the app immediately.
</Info>
